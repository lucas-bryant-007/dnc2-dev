## Result interpretation

Figure 1 uses 12 trained models: four pairing conditions at three seeds. The figure has 12 aggregated condition-task paths, not 12 factors. Exactly three paths are demoted and nine remain shared.

The natural boxes support a qualified claim. VICReg/CelebA is the clean positive example. I-JEPA visibly misses the fixed RMSE criterion. CUB supplies cross-dataset evidence. The all-attribute ECDF shows that selected low-overlap triples are not representative of every pair, so the paper should claim approximate geometry for selected train-feasible task sets rather than universal orthogonality.
